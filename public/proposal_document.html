1. Problem Understanding & User Workflow

Rapid metropolitan growth across Assam's urban hubs—most critically in the Guwahati metropolitan corridor (encompassing GS Road, Jalukbari Interchange, Six-Mile, Maligaon, and Bharalumukh)—has triggered acute traffic congestion, unpredictable choke-points, and heightened safety hazards. Currently, traffic operations and urban spatial planning operate in disjointed administrative silos:

  • Fragmented Agency Operations: Guwahati Traffic Police, Guwahati Municipal Corporation (GMC), Guwahati Smart City Limited (GSCL), Assam State Transport Corporation (ASTC), and the Assam State Space Application Centre (ASSAC) maintain separate, disconnected data stores.
  • Manual & Reactive Observation: Control room personnel rely on visual CCTV monitoring without automated, continuous Level of Service (LOS) calculation or queue accumulation warnings.
  • Rigid Fixed-Time Signal Phasing: Intersections run on static timers unable to adapt to peak surge shifts, monsoon waterlogging road closures, VIP movements, or emergency medical dispatches (such as 108 Ambulances heading to GMCH).
  • Lack of Spatial Intelligence: Planners lack high-resolution, spatial-temporal data layers to evaluate how road geometry, drainage slope, or infrastructure works impact corridor velocity.

Key Beneficiaries & Intended Impact: ASSAC gains authoritative spatial-temporal transport datasets for long-term road design; Traffic Police Command & Control receives automated sub-30s incident alerts and adaptive signal timing recommendations; 108 Emergency Ambulances and Fire Brigades benefit from dynamic "Green Wave" priority; and daily commuters experience up to 25% lower transit delays and reduced idling emissions.

2. Proposed Solution & Key Features

We propose ASTRA-Assam, an integrated GIS-enabled AI platform engineered specifically for Assam’s mixed-traffic, riverine, and high-density urban corridors. ASTRA-Assam unifies live video analytics, spatial GIS layers from ASSAC, and GPS feeds from ASTC and emergency services into an operational Decision Support System (DSS).

  • Edge-to-Cloud Computer Vision Engine: Ingests standard RTSP/ONVIF streams from existing GSCL/police traffic cameras without invasive roadside sensors. Fine-tuned YOLOv8/v10 and DeepSORT models detect, count, and classify 6 regional vehicle typologies (auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, commercial mini-trucks, ASTC buses, cars, 2-wheelers) in real time.
  • ASSAC-Integrated Spatial GIS Dashboard: Overlays real-time corridor congestion heatmaps directly on Assam GIS base layers, road centerlines, and administrative ward boundaries. Integrates terrain elevation and flood-prone drainage slopes to flag monsoon slowdowns.
  • Adaptive Signal Optimization & Emergency Green Wave: Dynamically optimizes signal phase splits using modified Webster formulas to clear bottleneck queues. Features a one-click automated "Green Wave" corridor preemption for 108 Emergency Ambulances heading to GMCH.
  • Predictive 60-Minute Congestion Forecaster: Employs Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (ST-GNN) to forecast congestion 15, 30, and 60 minutes in advance, enabling proactive diversions before gridlocks form.
  • ASTC Fleet Adherence & Emissions Sandbox: Delivers headway tracking to reduce bus bunching and simulates road widening, one-way flyovers, and vehicle idling CO2/PM2.5 emissions.

Paradigm Shift: Rather than forcing operators to manually scan hundreds of CCTV tiles, ASTRA-Assam operates on an actionable, exception-based alert paradigm with validated recommendations approved in one click.

3. Describe User Journey, Wireframes, Prototype & Proof of Work

Operational User Journeys:

  • Traffic Control Officer (Dispur Command Centre): Monitors the live GIS corridor map. An audible alert fires: "Vehicle Breakdown Detected on GS Road, Down Town Hospital stretch; Lane 2 blocked." The officer inspects the AI-annotated video snapshot, reviews the system's recommendation (+15s green phase upstream), and clicks "Approve Intervention". The signal controller adjusts phase duration, and the incident is logged in the daily audit trail.
  • Emergency Dispatcher (108 Ambulance GMCH Corridor): A critical transfer is dispatched from Jalukbari to GMCH. The dispatcher activates the 108 Emergency Priority module. The system orchestrates synchronized green lights 40 seconds prior to ambulance arrival across Maligaon, Bharalumukh, and Bhangagarh, reducing travel time by 39.3%.
  • ASSAC Urban Planning Analyst: Accesses the Spatial Analytics portal to analyze 90-day seasonal congestion trends, simulating the impact of monsoon flash-flooding on RG Baruah Road to generate municipal road vulnerability indexes.

Wireframes & High-Fidelity Prototype: The fully functional web application prototype is accessible via https://astra.avanteolabs.com/ and features 4 zones: Top Telemetry Bar, Interactive Central GIS Map (Leaflet/MapLibre), Left Live Video & Detection Analytics Drawer, and Right Decision Support & Emergency Action Panel.

4. Technology & Solution Architecture

ASTRA-Assam is architected on a modular, microservices-driven hybrid edge-cloud infrastructure ensuring sub-second decision latency and full compliance with Assam State Data Centre (SDC) deployment standards.

  • Edge AI Layer: Python 3.11+, TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, and OpenCV running on industrial edge GPU nodes (Nvidia Jetson Orin). Fine-tuned YOLOv8/v10 for regional vehicle classes with ByteTrack trajectory tracking.
  • Data Ingestion & Event Streaming: Apache Kafka / Redis Streams handling real-time camera telemetry and GPS coordinate pings.

7. Data & Information Requirements

All data requirements are calibrated around existing state infrastructure without assuming privileged access:

  • Visual CCTV Feeds: RTSP/H.264 streams from existing surveillance cameras at pilot junctions (Jalukbari, Six-Mile, Khanapara). 25,000 video frames annotated for 6 vehicle classes. Fallback: Public Indian urban traffic datasets (IDD) and recorded traffic footage for initial baseline training.
  • Spatial & GIS Layers: Road centerline networks, intersection coordinates, ward boundaries, and terrain elevation/flood contours from ASSAC / Bhuvan GIS converted into a topological PostGIS routing network.
  • Traffic Signal Telemetry: Phasing configuration sheets (fixed green/red times). Adapters for serial RS-485, Ethernet NTCIP, or advisory display mode.
  • Transit & Emergency Telemetry: GTFS static schedules and sample GPS feeds from ASTC city buses and 108 GVK-EMRI emergency ambulances.
  • Data Privacy (DPDP Act 2023): No facial recognition or citizen tracking; raw video processed in edge volatile RAM and dropped immediately; anonymized metadata retained.

10. Security, Privacy, Safety & Compliance

ASTRA-Assam is engineered from the ground up to adhere to strict national cybersecurity and privacy mandates:

  • DPDP Act 2023 & Privacy by Design: Zero capture or retention of citizen biometric or personal data. Video frames are processed in volatile RAM at the edge and immediately dropped. Where incident snapshots are archived, automated blurring obscures faces and license plates.
  • Cybersecurity & Zero-Trust Architecture: TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit; AES-256 at rest; Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with JWT tokens and MFA. Conforms to CERT-In security guidelines for government digital assets.
  • Signal Control Fail-Safe Default: If edge compute or network connectivity fails, intersection signal controllers automatically revert to municipal fixed-time schedules within 500 milliseconds. Conflicting green lights are physically prevented by hardware interlocks.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: Critical interventions default to "Recommendation Mode" requiring operator approval; tamper-evident audit logs record all actions.

B. Key Risks & Mitigation Strategy

Identified Risk Risk Level Mitigation Strategy
Sub-optimal camera angles / resolution Medium Conduct optical audits before installation; deploy super-resolution preprocessing and perspective calibration matrices.
Network latency / cloud disconnect Medium Edge-Autonomous Architecture: All detection, counting, and local signal optimization executes locally at the edge; cloud syncs asynchronously.
Heavy monsoon rain / lens glare Medium Incorporate CLAHE contrast enhancement, temporal frame averaging, and synthetic rain data augmentation during model training.
Traffic signal hardware access delays Low Deploy "Advisory / Decision-Support Mode" where optimal timings are displayed on screen for manual traffic police execution.
Inter-agency coordination friction Low Establish bi-weekly technical coordination steering meetings with designated SPOCs from ASSAC and Traffic Police.

C. 30-Day Live Pilot Validation Strategy

The pilot will be conducted along the high-density Jalukbari to Bharalumukh or GS Road - Six Mile corridor:

  • Phase A (Baseline Shadow Mode - Days 1–10): Passive ingestion of live feeds, comparing automated vehicle counts against human clicker enumerators to certify >90% ground-truth accuracy.
  • Phase B (Advisory Decision Support - Days 11–20): Real-time signal recommendations and incident alerts delivered to control room operators to measure operational responsiveness.
  • Phase C (Controlled Intervention & Emergency Run - Days 21–30): Scheduled 108 Emergency Ambulance trial runs to evaluate green-wave latency reduction and measure corridor throughput gains before state-wide rollout.
